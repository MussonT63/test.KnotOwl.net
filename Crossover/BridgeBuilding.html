The Bridge is the symbol of Crossover that connects the Cub Scouts to the Boys Scouts.

As the Scout begins the crossing he reads "Do Your Best", the motto of the Cub Scouts. The opposite side reads "Be Prepared" the motto of the Boy Scouts, and marks the beginning of a new adventure in Scouting, as a young adult.

To reach the other side your son has been learning the 12 points of the Scout Law. This law will not only guide him through Scouting, but also through life. He will do his best to live up to the Law. And it is not always easy to do, but a Scout always tries.

A Scout is TRUSTWORTHY:
A Scout tells the truth. He is honest, and people can depend on him.

A Scout is LOYAL:
A Scout is true to his family, friends, community, and nation.

A Scout is HELPFUL
A Scout cares about people, and volunteers to help others.

A Scout is FRIENDLY:
A Scout is a friend to all people of all nations.

A Scout is COURTEOUS:
A Scout is polite to everyone regardless of age or position.

A Scout is KIND:
A Scout is gentle, and treats others as he wants to be treated.

A Scout is OBEDIENT:
A Scout follows the rules and laws of his family, community and country.

A Scout is CHEERFUL:
A Scout looks for the bright side of life, and tries to make others happy.

A Scout is THRIFTY:
He saves for the future. He protects and conserves natural resources.

A Scout is BRAVE:
A Scout can face danger even though he is afraid and has the courage to stand for what he thinks is right.

A Scout is CLEAN:
A Scout keeps his mind and body fit. And helps keep his home and community clean.

A Scout is REVERENT:
A Scout is faithful in his religious beliefs toward God, and respects the beliefs of others.

These 12 points of the Scout Law complete the bridge, and make it ready to cross.
