Webelos to Boy Scouts Crossover Ceremony

Build the Bridge:
The Scout's Parents remove his webelos items:
  • Webelos neckerchief & slide.
  • Cub Scout blue shoulder loops.
The Cub Leader gives the Webelos a gift from the Pack and his last Cub Scout handshake.
The Webelos Scout crosses the bridge by himself.
The parents follow (behind the bridge) from the Cub side to the Boy Scout side of the bridge to show that they will activly support their son and his new Troop.
Boy Scout(s) put on a Boy Scout neckerchief & slide and Boy Scout shoulder loops.
The new Boy Scout is given his first Boy Scout handshake(s) by his new Troop mates and leaders.
The parents are welcomed by the new Boy Scout's Troop.
Symbology:
  • The parent removeing the Webelos items is symbolic of leaving the Cub Scout method of Scouting behind.
  • Crossing the bridge alone is symbolic of the boy leaving the Adult lead Pack and joining the Boy lead Troop.
  • The Boy Scouts putting on the Troop accoutrements is symbolic of entering the Boy Scout method of Scouting.
Notes:
  • One Webelos Scout and his parents at a time.
  • This simple ceremony can be expanded on considerably simply by adding readings of the symbology involved. This document has check-boxes next to all the elements. Any check box that is unchecked will not show up in the printable document after you pick the select button...
  • Anywhere the term parents is used it is indicating the Scouts responsible adult(s), ie. Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, etc. This can and should be multiple people if possible.
  • Anywhere the term Cub Leader is used it is indicating the Cubmaster, Webelos Den Leader, etc.
  • The gift from the Pack should be something the boy can use when he is in Boy Scouts. For example a Boy Scout Handbook or pocket knife - please check with the Troop for suggestions and or requirements.
