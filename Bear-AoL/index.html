Here is a plan (from 2010) to get your new Webelos Scouts from the end of their Bear year to Arrow of Light by their Crossover at the Pack Blue and Gold in February of their 5th grade!

The first thing you want to do is find out what priority the boys have for the optional Activity Pins.

I suggest you hand out this page and have the boys vote which ones they think are the coolest! That way you can put the least popular ones near the end (in case you run out of time to complete them.)

Remember, the goal is to cross all the boys into Boy Scouts with their Arrow of Light in February of their 5th grade so they have time to get familiar with their new Boy Scout Troop before going off to a week of summer camp.
