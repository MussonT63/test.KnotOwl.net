A word about Merit Badge Counselors in Chief Okemos District

The policy on how Badge Counselors (MBCs) are renewed on the District
Charter changed in the last few years. In the past the policy was "hey if you
DON'T want to continue, let us know".

Currently, an eMail in early May goes out saying you need to do
something to remain on the lists...

People seem to miss this eMail and as a result we are down from almost
400 to about 50 Merit Badge Counselors in Chief Okemos right now!

To get back on the list, you will need to submit new paperwork:

  • BSA Adult Application
  • Merit Badge Counselor Application
